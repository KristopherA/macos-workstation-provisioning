Operator config keys added in D8 and tightened in D9
APP_LIST belongs in both policy.conf and session.conf.
Format: Label/BundleName[/store] entries joined by ":".
Exact example: ExampleChat/ExampleChat:Example Sheets/Example Sheets/store.
Label and BundleName use [A-Za-z0-9 .+-]. BundleName is the .app name without the suffix.
Optional trailing /store marks an App Store app and is the only allowed third field.
wsp_app_list rejects the whole value when any empty part appears, including a trailing ":".
It also rejects an empty third field, a Label with leading or trailing spaces, and a BundleName that starts with "." or has leading or trailing spaces.
Unset or malformed APP_LIST makes the apps module and audit app-list check report BLOCKED_POLICY.
There is no built-in default app list.
Dock inventories take their approved apps from APP_LIST and operator config, not from hard-coded doc lists.
Module 5 installs only the non-store APP_LIST labels that also appear in APP_PACKAGES, and only from a local signed package in APP_SOURCE_DIR.

Operator config keys added in D16 (module 5) and D17 (module 6)
APP_PACKAGES, APP_TEAM_IDS, APP_SOURCE_DIR, and APP_INSTALL_CONSENT belong in trusted policy.conf only. The session file is never read for installs. Do not put a site value in the suite.
APP_PACKAGES uses Label=FILE entries separated by ":". Label must be a non-store APP_LIST label, exact, with spaces allowed, and at most one entry per label. FILE ends in .pkg or .dmg, uses only A-Za-z0-9, space, period, underscore, plus, or hyphen, has no "/", no leading "." or "-", no trailing space, and is at most 100 characters. Example: ExampleChat=ExampleChat-1.0.pkg:ExampleNotes=ExampleNotes 2.dmg.
APP_TEAM_IDS uses Label=TEAMID entries separated by ":" only. No commas, no spaces around the separator, and no bare Team IDs. TEAMID is exactly 10 characters from A-Z and 0-9. No duplicate label. Example: ExampleChat=ABCDE12345:ExampleNotes=FGHIJ67890. Those example Team IDs are fictional. Each Team ID is bound to its own label. Unset or empty APP_TEAM_IDS refuses apply (BLOCKED_POLICY).
APP_SOURCE_DIR is the operator's own absolute folder. Allowed characters are A-Za-z0-9, space, period, underscore, plus, hyphen, and slash. The value must not contain "..", must not contain "//", must not have a trailing slash, must not use a "." or ".." path segment, must be at most 200 characters, and must not be under /Applications, /Library/Management, or /System. The folder and every ancestor must be trusted.
